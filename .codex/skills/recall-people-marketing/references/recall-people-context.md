# Recall People Context

Use this as concise product context. Prefer current repository docs and code when exact facts matter.

## Product

Recall People helps users remember people they meet. The high-conversion explanation is:

`Say who you met. Recall People turns quick voice notes into names, context, reminders, and follow-up prompts.`

The app should feel useful for friends, colleagues, clients, conference contacts, family, neighbors, and casual acquaintances. Do not reduce the main category to "friends" unless the task is specifically social/friendship copy.

## Current Recommended Positioning

From `docs/marketing/aso-competitive-positioning-2026-05-20.md`:

- Main category: `AI people memory`.
- Main promise: `Never blank on a person again.`
- Support promise: `Say who you met. Recall People turns it into names, notes, reminders, and follow-ups.`
- Product enemy: the blank moment, forgotten details, missed follow-ups, and walking into conversations cold.
- First screenshot story: pain -> voice capture -> AI extraction -> prepared profile -> timely follow-up.

## Differentiators

- Voice-first capture after conversations.
- AI extraction of names, facts, dates, topics, and next actions.
- Contact profiles that become memory, not only address-book records.
- Day-before reminders, such as birthdays, interviews, trips, meetings.
- Next-morning follow-up prompts.
- Search by natural-language question across people and notes.
- Multi-language voice/copy support: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian.
- Local-first/private positioning, with optional sync. Verify exact privacy wording before publishing.

## Privacy and AI Claim Guardrails

Be careful with claims like `no third-party access ever`. The project has noted use of third-party AI services:

- Groq for voice transcription.
- Cerebras for text extraction, summaries, assistant, and semantic search.
- OpenAI GPT Image 2 for AI avatar generation from text descriptions.
- User content is described as locally stored; backend is described as stateless and not persisting user content.

Safer copy patterns:

- `Your data is stored locally on your device.`
- `You review what Recall People saves.`
- `Optional encrypted sync keeps your memory available across devices.`
- `AI helps extract details from your notes; you stay in control.`

Avoid unless re-verified:

- `No third-party access, ever.`
- `100% private.`
- `Never sends anything off-device.`
- `AI remembers everything automatically.`

## Audience Segments

Use one dominant segment per asset.

- Social/thoughtful: wants to be the person who remembers birthdays, trips, interviews, family details.
- Professional/networking: meets many people at work, conferences, founder events, sales calls, freelance/client contexts.
- Memory-anxious: feels awkward forgetting names and context.
- Relationship maintenance: wants to reconnect naturally and avoid letting people drift.
- Power user: wants semantic search and summaries across a personal network.

## Strong Angles

- Shame relief: `Never blank on a person again.`
- Effortless capture: `Talk for 15 seconds after you meet someone.`
- Thoughtfulness: `Follow up when it actually matters.`
- Preparedness: `Walk into every conversation with context.`
- Search: `Ask your network anything.`
- Control: `AI organizes the note, you decide what stays.`

## Weak Angles

- Generic `AI contact manager` without a human moment.
- `Personal CRM` as the only story for a consumer audience.
- Feature piles: notes, contacts, reminders, search, AI, avatars, sync, languages all at once.
- Overly broad self-improvement copy like `build better relationships`.
- Copy that implies surveillance or automated memory without consent/control.
