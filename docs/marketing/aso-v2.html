Le repositionnement

4 décisions structurantes qui guident toute la V2.

1 · CRM Personnel → Friend Memory

Le hero passe de pro/froid à émotionnel/aspirationnel. "CRM personnel" reste dans les mots-clés (SEO) mais quitte le nom et le sous-titre.

2 · Les notifs deviennent un argument central

Timing exact extrait du code (lib/notification-schedule.ts) : veille à 19h + lendemain à 10h. Plus crédible que "24h avant".

3 · Screens 4 & 5 refaits (murs de texte → 1 visuel fort)

Recadrage du mockup phone + overlay notif iOS native (screen 4) ou sticker matinal (screen 5). Fini l'overwhelm.

4 · Rythme chromatique (3 ruptures violettes)

Screens 2, 5, 8 passent en violet brand plein. L'œil accroche au scroll App Store au lieu de 8 écrans gris-pâle.

HOW IT WORKS

Just talk. After any coffee, call, or dinner, open Recall and tell it:

"Just saw Sarah. She's interviewing at Google next week, and she's finally moving to that new apartment in Brooklyn on the 15th."

Done. Recall handles the rest:

✓ Extracts names, facts, and dates automatically
✓ Creates or updates Sarah's contact profile
✓ Schedules a reminder the day before each event at 7pm
✓ Nudges you the next morning to follow up
✓ Generates an AI summary of who they really are

No typing. No organizing. Just talking.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

◆ NOTIFIED THE DAY BEFORE, AT 7 PM
Most apps remind you the day of — too late. Recall pings you the evening before. The perfect moment to draft a message, grab a gift, prepare a thought. "Good luck tomorrow, I'm rooting for you."

◆ FOLLOW-UP NUDGE THE NEXT MORNING
The morning after an event, Recall whispers: "Ask Sarah how her interview went." You naturally become the thoughtful one.

◆ VOICE-FIRST CAPTURE
Notes in seconds. 5 languages — English, French, Spanish, German, Italian.

◆ CONTACT PROFILES THAT FILL THEMSELVES
Job, family, hobbies, projects, latest topics — all in one place. Plus an AI summary that tells you who they really are in 3 lines.

◆ SEARCH BY QUESTION
"Who has kids?" "Who should I reconnect with this month?" — just describe.

◆ WEEKLY DIGEST (PRO)
Every Monday at 9am, Recall sums up the week ahead in your circle.

◆ PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your data is stored locally and encrypted. Optional sync across your devices. No third-party access, ever.

Checklist d'exécution

  • Créer 8 frames Figma 1290 × 2796 (iPhone 16 Pro Max)
  • Appliquer les fonds selon la table rythmique (3 violets, 4 clairs, 1 glow)
  • Importer les mockups depuis ASO-FINAL-XX.png
  • Screen 4 : créer composant overlay notif iOS native + recadrer mockup
  • Screen 5 : recadrer mockup pour 1 question en gros + sticker jaune 🌅 10:00 AM
  • Screens 1, 3, 6 : ajouter stickers/callouts colorés
  • Vérifier chaque headline ≤ 2 lignes en preview iPhone
  • Exporter PNG 1290 × 2796 ou HEIC pour App Store Connect
  • Test utilisateur : "qu'est-ce que fait cette app ?" après 5s de scroll
  • Mettre à jour App Store Connect (FR + EN) avec wording V2
