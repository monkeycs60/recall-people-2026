"So — how did your interview go last week?"

Be that person. The one who remembers. Every meet-up, trip, birthday, project of the people in your life.

Recall People nudges you the day before what matters to them — at the right moment, with the right question.

══════════════════════════════

THE PROBLEM

We forget 80% of any conversation within 24 hours. Marcus's marathon, Sarah's Google interview, Emma's move — gone.

Yet those small details are exactly what makes someone feel cared for.

══════════════════════════════

HOW IT WORKS

Just talk. After any coffee, call, or dinner, open Recall and tell it:

"Coffee with Sarah, she's interviewing at Google next week and moving to Brooklyn on the 15th."

Done. Recall handles the rest:

✓ Extracts names, facts, and dates automatically
✓ Creates or updates Sarah's contact profile
✓ Reminds you the day before each event at 7pm
✓ Nudges you the next morning to follow up
✓ AI summary of who they really are

No typing. No organizing. Just talking.

══════════════════════════════

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

★ NOTIFIED THE DAY BEFORE, AT 7 PM
Not the day-of (too late). The evening before: the right moment to draft a message, grab a gift.

★ FOLLOW-UP NUDGE THE NEXT MORNING
The morning after an event, Recall whispers: "Ask Sarah how her interview went." You become the thoughtful one — naturally.

★ VOICE-FIRST CAPTURE
Notes in seconds. 5 languages — English, French, Spanish, German, Italian.

★ AUTO-FILLED CONTACT PROFILES
Job, family, hobbies, projects — all in one place. Plus a 3-line AI summary that tells you who they really are.

★ SEARCH BY QUESTION
"Who has kids?" "People I met at the conference" — just describe.

★ WEEKLY DIGEST (PRO)
Every Monday at 9am, a recap of the week ahead in your circle.

★ PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Encrypted local storage. Optional sync across your devices. No third-party access, ever.
