## General Recording Tips / Conseils generaux de tournage

### EN - Recording Tips

**Device Settings:**
- Enable **Do Not Disturb** mode (Settings > Focus > Do Not Disturb) to block all notifications
- Set battery to **100%** (or at least 80%+) - the battery icon should look full
- Set the time to **9:41** (Apple's iconic marketing time): Settings > General > Date & Time > turn off "Set Automatically" > set to 9:41 AM
- Connect to **Wi-Fi** with full bars (or use a strong Wi-Fi network name like "Home")
- Set cellular signal to full bars (if visible)
- Set the **language to English** (or French for the FR version) in the app settings

**Screen Recording Method:**
- Use the **iOS built-in screen recorder**: Control Center > Screen Recording button
- Before recording: go to Settings > Control Center > add "Screen Recording" if not already there
- Long-press the Screen Recording button and enable **Microphone Audio** if you want to capture voice narration OR the voice note audio
- Resolution will automatically be captured at the device's native resolution

**Important: for the voice note audio:**
- The voice note you record IN the app during the demo will be captured by the screen recorder if microphone is on
- This becomes the natural audio track for Video 1
- For Videos 2 and 3, you can record silently and add music in post

**Screen Recording Tips:**
- Tap the red recording indicator to stop recording
- The video saves to Photos automatically
- Trim the start (3-second countdown) and end in Photos before exporting

**Performance Tips:**
- Close all other apps (swipe up from bottom, swipe away all apps)
- Restart the iPhone before recording for best performance
- Make sure the app is freshly launched with demo data loaded
- Practice the flow 2-3 times before the real recording
- Use smooth, deliberate finger movements - not too fast, not too slow
- Pause slightly (~0.5s) after each tap to let the UI respond and animate

### FR - Conseils de tournage

**Reglages de l'appareil :**
- Activer le mode **Ne pas deranger** (Reglages > Concentration > Ne pas deranger) pour bloquer toutes les notifications
- Charger la batterie a **100%** (ou au moins 80%+) - l'icone de batterie doit paraitre pleine
- Regler l'heure a **9:41** (l'heure iconique du marketing Apple) : Reglages > General > Date et heure > desactiver "Reglage automatique" > regler a 9h41
- Se connecter a un **Wi-Fi** avec plein de barres
- Signal cellulaire a plein de barres (si visible)
- Regler la **langue en francais** (ou anglais pour la version EN) dans les reglages de l'app

**Methode d'enregistrement d'ecran :**
- Utiliser l'**enregistreur d'ecran iOS integre** : Centre de controle > bouton Enregistrement d'ecran
- Avant l'enregistrement : Reglages > Centre de controle > ajouter "Enregistrement de l'ecran" si pas deja present
- Appui long sur le bouton d'enregistrement et activer l'**Audio du Microphone** si vous souhaitez capturer la narration vocale OU l'audio de la note vocale
- La resolution sera automatiquement celle de l'appareil

**Important pour l'audio de la note vocale :**
- La note vocale que vous enregistrez DANS l'app pendant la demo sera captee par l'enregistreur d'ecran si le microphone est actif
- Cela devient la piste audio naturelle de la Video 1
- Pour les Videos 2 et 3, vous pouvez enregistrer en silence et ajouter de la musique en post-production

**Conseils de performance :**
- Fermer toutes les autres apps
- Redemarrer l'iPhone avant l'enregistrement
- S'assurer que l'app est fraichement lancee avec les donnees demo chargees
- Pratiquer le flux 2-3 fois avant le vrai enregistrement
- Utiliser des mouvements de doigt fluides et deliberes
- Faire une courte pause (~0.5s) apres chaque tap pour laisser l'UI reagir et animer

## Pre-recording Checklist / Checklist avant tournage

- [ ] Do Not Disturb ON

- [ ] iPhone restarted recently

## Post-production Notes / Notes de post-production

### EN - Post-production

**Trimming:**
- Trim the 3-second countdown at the beginning of each screen recording
- Trim any fumbles or hesitations at the end
- Remove dead air (moments where nothing happens for more than 1 second)
- Target final duration: 25-30 seconds per video (App Store allows 15-30s)

**Text Overlays:**
- Use a clean, modern sans-serif font (SF Pro, Inter, or Helvetica Neue)
- White text with a subtle shadow or dark semi-transparent background pill
- Overlays should appear for 2-3 seconds, then fade out
- Position: centered horizontally, upper third of the screen (above the main action area)
- Keep text SHORT - 5 words maximum per overlay
- Use the overlays suggested in each script's table

**Recommended Tools:**
- **CapCut** (free, mobile) - best for quick text overlays and transitions
- **iMovie** (free, Mac/iOS) - simple but effective
- **Canva Video** (free tier) - good templates for text overlays
- **Final Cut Pro** (Mac) - if you want the highest quality

**Transitions:**
- Use simple **cross-dissolve** transitions between scenes (0.3-0.5s)
- Do NOT use flashy transitions (no zooms, spins, wipes)
- The video should feel smooth and natural, like watching someone actually use the app

**Music (for Videos 2 & 3 only):**
- Video 1 uses the actual voice recording audio - no music needed
- For Videos 2 & 3, add subtle background music:
  - Style: upbeat but calm, modern, minimal (think Apple keynote vibes)
  - Tempo: ~100-120 BPM
  - Royalty-free sources:
    - Artlist.io (paid, high quality)
    - Epidemic Sound (paid, high quality)
    - YouTube Audio Library (free)
    - Pixabay Music (free)
    - Uppbeat.io (free with attribution)
  - Suggested search terms: "minimal tech", "clean corporate", "soft upbeat", "modern positive"
  - Volume: keep at ~20-30% so it doesn't overpower the visuals
  - Fade in at the start, fade out at the end (1-2 seconds each)

**End Card (optional but recommended):**
- After the last frame, add a 2-3 second end card with:
  - App icon centered
  - "Recall People" text below
  - Tagline: "Remember every conversation" / "N'oubliez plus vos rencontres"
  - Background: the app's primary color or a clean gradient

**Final Export Settings:**
- **Resolution:** 1290 x 2796 pixels (iPhone 15 Pro Max) or 1284 x 2778 (iPhone 14 Pro Max). Check your device's native resolution.
- **Format:** H.264 MP4 or MOV
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds (App Store requirement)
- **File size:** under 500 MB (App Store limit)
- **Audio:** AAC, 48 kHz (for Video 1 with voice audio)
- **No letterboxing:** the video must fill the entire screen, no black bars

**Apple App Store Preview Requirements:**
- You need previews for each device size you support:
  - 6.7" display (iPhone 15 Pro Max / 14 Pro Max): 1290 x 2796 or 1284 x 2778
  - 6.5" display (iPhone 11 Pro Max / XS Max): 1242 x 2688
  - 5.5" display (iPhone 8 Plus): 1080 x 1920
- You can upload up to 3 preview videos per localization
- Record on the largest device and scale down for smaller sizes
- Each video must be 15-30 seconds long
