# ASO Screenshot Selection - Recall People

Date: 2026-06-06

Source folder: `/home/clement/Desktop/Recall People screenshots final 2026-06-02`

## Assumption

Primary target is the iOS App Store English listing. The screenshots are in English, so the proposed captions are English first. French localization should use a separate translated screenshot set, not mixed-language overlays.

## Strategy

The first three screenshots must tell the full conversion story:

1. The pain and promise: people, details, and follow-ups are easy to forget.
2. The mechanism: speak after meeting someone.
3. The result: a useful memory profile with upcoming moments and context.

Then the remaining screenshots prove reminders, follow-up prompts, search, catch-up workflow, and language coverage.

Avoid positioning the whole app only as a friendship app. Use the warmer emotional tone, but keep the category broad enough for friends, colleagues, clients, events, and networking.

## Production Notes

- Put large overlay copy above the phone. Do not rely on small in-app text.
- Keep one idea per screenshot. If the UI is dense, add one sticker/callout that tells the user what to notice.
- Alternate light warm backgrounds and full brand-purple backgrounds to avoid eight pale screens in a row.
- If using a free-trial claim, verify current pricing before export.
- Avoid privacy copy like "no third-party access ever" unless implementation and third-party AI usage have been re-verified.

## Exclude From Primary App Store Set

These are not bad screenshots, but they are weaker for acquisition:

- `03-contacts-manage-groups-sheet.png`: secondary organization feature.
- `04-contacts-new-contact-modal.png`: setup/admin, not value.
- `06-contact-options-menu.png`: menu state, no conversion story.
- `08-contact-all-notes.png`: does not clearly show notes at App Store size.
- `11-record-type.png`: text input is less differentiated than voice.
- `13-news-past.png`: past events are less compelling than future action.
- `15-profile-top.png`, `17-profile-lower.png`, `18-profile-option-sheet.png`: settings/subscription/test-mode surfaces.
- `23-overdue-resolution-filled-not-sent.png`: too late in workflow and too specific.
- `24-contact-sort-sheet.png`: utility setting, not a core promise.

## Optional Variants To Test

### Broad Memory Variant

Use this as the default:

- Screenshot 1 headline: `Never blank on a person again`
- App subtitle direction: `Names, notes & follow-ups`

This is the safest broad-market version because it covers friends, colleagues, clients, and networking.

### Friend/Thoughtful Variant

- Screenshot 1 headline: `Be the one who remembers`
- Screenshot 5 headline: `Ask the thoughtful question`

This may convert well for social users, but it risks making the app feel less useful professionally.

### Pro/Networking Variant

- Screenshot 1 headline: `Remember everyone you meet`
- Screenshot 6 headline: `Search your whole network`

This is better for Apple Ads or a custom product page targeting networking/founder/freelance traffic.
